You should always choose a delivery based on the destination. If you have to deliver a car to a location where you cannot get another delivery, you will have to call a taxi to relocate, and that comes with great tedium.

> "Good" destination: Jobs finish at Fast, Rich, Belasco Auto, or similarly good locations.
> "Bad" destination: Jobs are either dead-end, or finish at other "Bad" locations; rare "Good" exceptions.
> "Bad+" destination: Bad default pool, but has higher than average "Good" exceptions.
> "Special" destination: For late-game work vehicles. These can transport to conventional "Good" locations (like Fast or Rich), but also amongst themselves.
> High-End Cars: 3 to 7 thousand.
> Mixed Cars: 800 to 1500 average.
> Low-End Cars: Under ~500
> Work Vehicles: Highest paying, but unconventional vehicles.
