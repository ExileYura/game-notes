You should always choose a delivery based on the destination. If you have to deliver a car to a location where you cannot get another delivery, you will have to call a taxi to relocate, and that comes with great tedium.
Below are lists of destinations, and my experiences with them.

| Car Dealerships       | Expectations   | Comment                                                |
| --------------------- | -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Fast Automotive       | High End       | Jobs start after lv.22 Logistics - no jobs until then. |
| Rich's Motor Company  | High End       | Jobs start after lv.22 Logistics - no jobs until then. |
| Jefferson Motors      | Medium Range   |                                                        |
| Belasco Auto          | > Medium Range |                                                        |
| Gizamn's Rod Shop     | > Medium Range |                                                        |
| Smash Rollers         | > Low End      |                                                        |
| Trusted Auto Sales    | > Low End      |                                                        |
| Quarryside Auto Sales | > Low End      |                                                        |

> Dealerships usually have jobs that takes you to other dealerships - try to take these when possible to stay in a comfortable loop.
