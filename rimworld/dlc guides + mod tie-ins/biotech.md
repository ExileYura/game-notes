# Mechanoids

Mechanoids are dickheads that attack your colony occasionally. They are an all-around upgrade to human raids. You can also control them. Below is the general work-flow on how to become a Mech Overlord.

|                                                              |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |     |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --- |
| Obtain a [Mechlink](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Mechlink). | Destroy [Ancient Exostrider Midsection](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Ancient_exostrider_midsection) that is on your starting map. This will yield a Transponder, which - if you decrypt it at the Research bench - will allow you to call down a Mechanoid Ship, where you can obtain a Mechlink if you successfully defeat it. (Destroying the Midsection in itself doesn't summon any mechs, and the Quest given by the decryption of the transponder does not expire, so it should be done as early as possible to avoid losing access to it later.) |

1. Becoming a Mechanitor

   - Acquiring a mechlink
   - Choosing a suitable colonist
   - Mech bandwidth and control

2) Basic Mechtech

   - Research requirements
   - Mech gestator and subcore encoder
   - First mechanoids
   - Basic labor and combat roles

3. Mechanoid Infrastructure

   - Charging and power
   - Repair and resurrection
   - Subcore production
   - Pollution and wastepack management

4. Expanding the Workforce

   - Standard Mechtech and advanced research
   - Large gestator and recharger
   - More specialized mechanoids
   - Increasing bandwidth

5. Advanced Mechtech

   - Acquiring advanced technology
   - High-tier mechanoids
   - Advanced mechanitor upgrades

   - Useful combinations with other mods
