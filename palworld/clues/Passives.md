HOW GET

Natural
→ Catch/hatch normally; passive can randomly appear.

Mutation
→ Breed for a mutated egg (1%, or 3% with Extravagant Vegetable Cake).

World Tree
→ Obtain from a World Tree Pal or its corresponding Disposable Implant.

Exclusive
→ Obtain the specific Pal/variant/encounter associated with the passive.

Surgery
→ Obtain the passive's Implant and apply it at the Pal Surgery Table.

Special encounter
→ Obtain the specific Pal/encounter that carries the passive.
