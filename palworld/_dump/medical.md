# Ailments

| Ailment   | Penalty                          | Cure                        |
| --------- | -------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Cold      | Work Speed ↓                     | Low Grade Medical Supplies  |
| Sprain    | Movement Speed ↓                 | Low Grade Medical Supplies  |
| Overfull  | Hunger depletion ↑               | Low Grade Medical Supplies  |
| Ulcer     | Work Speed ↓; Movement Speed ↓   | Medical Supplies            |
| Fracture  | Work Speed ↓; Movement Speed ↓   | Medical Supplies            |
| Weakened  | Work Speed ↓↓; Movement Speed ↓↓ | High Grade Medical Supplies |
| Depressed | Work Speed ↓↓; Movement Speed ↓↓ | High Grade Medical Supplies |

# Medicine

| Medicine                    | Cures                  | Ingredients                | Merchant   |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------- | -------------------------- | ---------- |
| Low Grade Medical Supplies  | Cold, Sprain, Overfull | Red Berries x5; Horn x2    | 780 Gold   |
| Medical Supplies            | Ulcer, Fracture        | Ingot x3; Horn x3; Bone x1 | 1,820 Gold |
| High Grade Medical Supplies | Weakened, Depressed    | Ingot x5; Horn x5; Bone x2 | 3,120 Gold |

> Horns (200G) can be bought from a variety of merchants; easiest is at Fisherman's Point - this merchant also sells the medicines.
> All medicine can be produced at all three (Mediecal, Electric, Advanced) work stations.

# Prevention

> Handle Sanity and Hunger stats.
> Objects that can help in prevention: Clinic (req. medicine production suitability), Hot Springs, Quality Beds, Ancient Monitoring Stand (late-game), Alpha Wave Generator
> Avoid overworking - control at Monitoring Stand.
> There are also some foods that can help with sanity (notably Mozzarina Hamburger, which is also the best Work Speed food), but these usually require meat, so they cannot be automatically farmed unless you catch a Legendary Game Hunter (very tedious).
