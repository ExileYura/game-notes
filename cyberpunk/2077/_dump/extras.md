This page contains small pieces of information regarding quests, that are very useful to know. I will not include spoilers here.

- The Pickup:
  - Nomads can talk to Jackie about his bike when meeting in front of All Foods, and it is recommended to do so.
  - NPC Brick can be saved during this quest, and he will show up in later quests if saved here. Code to his cell is in the room next door.
