This page contains quests that are missable depending on the choices you make in other quests. Mild spoilers.

- The Pickup

  - Mission 'Venus in Furs' is missable.
  - This is a short inconsequential mission, and completely skippable. It does unlock a very cool unique weapon though.
  - Choices to make during The Pickup:

    - Meet Meredith.
    - Don't Decrypt the Chip.
    - Pay with the Chip during the mission.
    - Defeat Maelstrom.
    - Meredith with send you a message later. You need to flirt with her to unlock the quest.
